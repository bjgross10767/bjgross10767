# Brandon Gross

I was a dual major in accounting and information systems, so I have always pursued technology grounded in business needs.

I got my start as an analyst, defining business processes, and soon moved into development. Over time the roles stacked up rather than replaced each other. On large data projects I was often the analyst, architect, lead developer and project manager at once, leading teams of developers and testers and carrying the work from requirements through design, build, test, deployment and support. Holding those roles gave me a high degree of control and let me make sure each project delivered the business value it was meant to.

Across 26 years the specialty has stayed the same:

- **Integrations,** internal and B2B
- **Application development**
- **Operational and warehouse data stores**

Along the way I was a Microsoft MVP for enterprise integration (2004 to 2006) and published two books on BizTalk Server.

**What I work on now**

- **Context models:** ontologies and process models that help AI understand how a business works and how the work actually gets done.
- **Agentic coding:** building software with AI agents as part of the daily workflow.
